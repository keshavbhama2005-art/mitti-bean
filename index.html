From Soil to Cup

We know the farmer before we know the flavor notes.

Twice a year, our roaster travels to family-run estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg to pick lots by hand. What comes back gets roasted within ten days of arrival, in batches small enough that the person pulling your shot could probably tell you the farmer's name.
